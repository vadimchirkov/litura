Stilura for Chrome

Rewrite, generate and keep writing in text fields.

Start the local Stilura app, then connect.

Select text and describe the change. Type /instructions, then Enter to generate. ⌘/Ctrl + Enter also works and requests a continuation without a selection. Tab accepts, R or Alt + Enter regenerates, ↑/↓ switches variants, Esc dismisses.

Text from the active field goes to your configured model. Password fields and fields marked for payment details are excluded. Reconnect after restarting the app.
